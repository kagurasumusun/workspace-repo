Types may include missing source family, unexplored version, unexplored architecture/ABI, unexplored toolchain, unexplored artifact category, unresolved contradiction, candidate with insufficient evidence, implementation gap, verification gap, or provenance/rights gap.

A gap is not cleared merely because an implementation was added. Relevant evidence and applicability must also be addressed.
