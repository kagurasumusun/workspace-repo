# Rights, Licensing, and Clean-Room Boundaries

Operational framework, not legal advice.

Track independently:

- access/permission to retrieve
- license/reuse rights
- privacy/personal-data constraints
- security constraints
- distribution rights
- jurisdiction/time
- contractual/platform restrictions
- research usefulness

Do not infer `public ⇒ free to reuse` or `robots.txt ⇒ legal authorization/prohibition`.

## Workflow statuses

`proceed | proceed_with_conditions | hold_for_review | reject_action | rights_unknown | permission_required | counsel_review | cleared_for_intended_use | cleared_with_conditions | likely_permitted_but_verify`

## Reverse engineering

Record whether a fact came from observation, documentation, external behavior, restricted material, or independent implementation. Where a clean-room process applies, keep restricted material and implementation activity segregated as required by the project and applicable law.

Evaluate observation, implementation, and distribution separately.
