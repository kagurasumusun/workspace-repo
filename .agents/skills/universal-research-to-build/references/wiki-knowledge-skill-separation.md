# Experience, Persistent Knowledge, and Skill

Keep three layers distinct:

`raw experience/trajectory → persistent knowledge → executable Skill`

Raw experience is evidence. Persistent knowledge is consolidated reusable information. A Skill is an executable procedure with routing and applicability boundaries.

Do not promote optimization history directly into instructions. Consolidate repeated evidence first; then propose a minimal Skill change with a verification plan.

Research such as WikiSkill supports the separation of experience, accumulated knowledge, and executable skills, with persistent knowledge acting as a substrate for later Skill evolution.
